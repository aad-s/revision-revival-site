About the movement

Hifdh is a gift.
Revision sustains it.

Revision Revival exists to renew the culture of Qur’an revision and elevate the homegrown huffadh serving Australian communities.

Memorising the Qur’an is not a finish line. It is a lifelong trust that needs rhythm, companionship and spaces where recitation is taken seriously. Our gatherings place huffadh at the heart of the day while giving the wider community a meaningful role in listening, following and encouraging.

Our Sydney launch proved the model: a complete khatmah recited across one day, sustained by committed readers, attentive listeners and a community that stayed present from morning until night. Melbourne is the next step in building an enduring national movement—one city, one gathering and one revived relationship with the Qur’an at a time.

The event structure

A full day built around the Qur’an.

A focused format that gives readers the conditions to revise well and gives every attendee a genuine part in the khatmah.

01

Recitation, side by side

Huffadh carry the complete khatmah in scheduled reading blocks, supported by paired listeners who follow closely and record corrections.

02

Guidance that renews

Short reminders and guest speakers reconnect revision to sincerity, discipline and the lifelong responsibility of carrying Allah’s Book.

03

Hospitality throughout

Meals and refreshments are provided so participants can remain present, build brotherhood and sustain their energy across the day.
